# 💖 Her Daily Affirmations 🌸

A beautifully designed Flutter app created with love to inspire, empower, and uplift women daily through powerful affirmations, gentle themes, and magical experiences.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🌈 **Daily Affirmations**
  - Inspiring quotes delivered daily to uplift your mood.
  - Elegant quote UI with custom fonts and layouts.

- 🎨 **Dark & Light Mode**
  - Automatically adjusts to your system theme.
  - Calming, feminine design in both modes.

- 🕰️ **Custom Reminders**
  - Set personalized reminder times for your quotes.
  - Uses local notifications to gently alert you with messages.

- 🎭 **Themes**
  - Choose from multiple beautiful themes to match your vibe.

- 🌸 **Magics Section**
  - A unique collection of interactive, spiritual, and relaxing features:
    - Camera + Quote frame
    - Mind’s Eye mode (with selfie view + quote)
    - Audio experiences
    - Games for emotional well-being
    - Much more coming soon...

- 🧠 **Animations & Transitions**
  - Smooth, cute transitions between screens using Flutter animations.

- 🎵 **Music Integration**
  - Background sounds for meditation or focus (optional).

- 📅 **Quote Scheduler**
  - View your upcoming affirmation schedule and manage it easily.

- 🔒 **Ad-Free & Lightweight**
  - Pure experience with no distractions.
